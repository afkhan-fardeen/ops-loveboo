# Marketing guide

For everyone on the team. The same guide is in the portal under **Marketing → Guide**.

One place to see **what is happening when**, who owns it, when it is due, and where the files are.

## Start here
**I want to see what's on** (everyone)
- Open **What's on** for campaigns running now and coming up, and what is due this week.
- Open **Calendar** and click anything for the details, the owner and the Drive links.
- Use the filters (campaign, type, status, owner, market) to see only what matters to you.

**I plan and update the work** (people who can edit)
- Press **New campaign**, then add a **New deliverable** for each banner, post or email.
- Change a status in one click as the work moves (click the status badge).
- On the Calendar, click a day to add something, or drag things to reschedule.

**I look after the team** (admins)
- Open the **Team** tab and press **Add member**: name, email, a generated password.
- Choose what each person can do in Marketing: no access, can view, or can view and edit.
- Share the sign-in details privately. Use **Users** to disable someone or give Reports or Events access.

## The portal at a glance
- **Sign in** at ops.loveboo.com with the email and password an admin gave you. The menu on the left (tabs at the bottom on a phone) shows only the areas you can open.
- **On your phone:** open ops.loveboo.com, then use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" (Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen; Chrome: menu → Install app).
- **Forgot your password?** Ask an admin to set a new one for you in Users.

## The idea in 30 seconds
- A **campaign or event** is the big thing with dates, for example *Black Friday, 24–30 Nov*. It shows on the calendar as a coloured band.
- **Deliverables** are the jobs inside it: a banner, a social post, an email, an ad. Each has a **Due** date (when the work is needed), a **Goes live** date (when it is published), an owner, a status and its Drive links.
- Everyone sees the same plan, so nobody has to ask "what's happening this week?".

## Getting started (people who can edit)
1. Press **New campaign** (top right). Give it a name, type, start and end dates, a colour, an owner, the markets, the offer or code, a short brief and the Drive folder link. Save.
2. On the campaign page, press **New deliverable** for each banner, post or email. Set *Due* and *Goes live*, pick the owner, and add the Drive link to the file or folder.
3. **Update the status** as the work moves. Click the status badge anywhere to change it in one step.
4. Check **What's on** each morning: overdue work (red), due in the next 7 days, going live in the next 7 days.

Switch with the buttons at the top right (a dropdown on a phone). It remembers your last view. **Colour by** colours deliverables by campaign, type, status or owner.

| View | Good for |

## The email plan
Every promotional email on its send date, so we can plan ahead and everyone knows what is going out and which products to push.
1. **Plan ahead** (people who can edit): choose the days you send (Sunday and Thursday are pre-ticked) and how far ahead, then press *Add emails*. Each send appears as an empty slot on its date.
2. Press **Edit** on an email. Give it a **theme** (for example *Mums & Babies*), then add the **products to push** (name, a link to the product, and a note if useful), the **copy** or a link to the doc, the owner and the status.
3. Everyone can see the plan. The **Products** tab shows each product and the dates it is planned, and flags a product planned twice within a week.

Email statuses: **To do** nothing ready yet, **In progress** copy or design being made, **In review**, **Ready** scheduled in Klaviyo, **Live** sending today, **Done** sent. Emails also show on the Calendar and can be dragged to another send day. Anyone who should add products or copy needs **Can view and edit**.

## Statuses
| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **To do** | Not started. |
| **In progress** | Being made. |
| **In review** | Made, waiting for feedback or approval. |
| **Ready** | Approved, waiting for its go-live date. |
| **Live** | Published. |
| **Done** | Finished and wrapped up. |

A deliverable is **overdue** when its due date has passed and it is still *To do*, *In progress* or *In review*. *Ready*, *Live* and *Done* are never overdue.

## Drive links
- Paste the link to the file or folder and give it a clear label, for example *Banners, final* or *Reel brief*. It shows as a chip (Drive folder, Google Doc, Sheet, Slides, Canva, Figma…).
- The portal only stores the link. **Google Drive decides who can open it**, so set the folder to "Anyone at Love Boo with the link", or teammates will see Google's request-access page.
- Tip: keep one Drive folder per campaign and put that folder on the campaign itself.

## Markets
Add the countries or regions a campaign runs in as tags. Pick from the suggestions or type any country and press Enter. Use the *Market* filter on the calendar to see one market at a time.

## Who can do what
- **Can view:** sees everything in Marketing.
- **Can view and edit:** can also add and change campaigns and deliverables.
- **Admins** always can edit. An admin chooses each person's access in the **Team** tab (or in Users).

### For admins: adding a member
1. Marketing → **Team** → **Add member**.
2. Enter their name and work email, press the wand to generate a password, and choose **Can view** or **Can view and edit**.
3. Share the sign-in details with them privately. The password is not shown again.
4. To change someone's access later, use the dropdown next to their name. To disable a person or give access to Reports or Events, use **Users**.

## Tips
- Add one-day moments (Singles Day, Mother's Day in a market) as the type **Key date**.
- Keep deliverable titles specific, for example "Homepage banner, desktop + mobile", so they are easy to find with the search box.
- Set the **owner** on everything: it is what makes **My tasks** useful.

## Questions people ask
- **I can't see Marketing in the menu.** An admin needs to give you access: Marketing → Team, set you to "Can view" or "Can view and edit".
- **I can see Marketing but can't add or change anything.** You have view access. Ask an admin for "Can view and edit".
- **A Drive link asks me to request access.** Google Drive decides who can open a file, not the portal. Ask the file's owner to share it with "Anyone at Love Boo with the link", or with you.
- **I dragged something to the wrong day.** Drag it back, or open it and change the date. Nothing is lost.
- **It won't let me move a deliverable.** A deliverable can't go live before it is due. Change its due date first, or move the go-live day later.
- **I deleted a campaign by mistake.** There is no undo, and deleting a campaign also deletes its deliverables. To drop a campaign but keep the record, set its status to *Cancelled*: it stays on the calendar, struck through.
- **Why is something marked overdue?** Its due date has passed and it is still To do, In progress or In review. Move it to Ready, Live or Done and the red goes away.
- **The date looks a day off.** Dates are plain calendar dates with no time of day. "Today", overdue and countdowns follow Bahrain time.
- **The page says it couldn't load.** Refresh once. If it keeps happening, tell an admin what you were opening.
